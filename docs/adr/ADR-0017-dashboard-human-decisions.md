# ADR-0017 — Dashboard centered on human decisions and exceptions

## Status
Accepted

## Decision
The dashboard is optimized around the two mandatory human gates, Recording Pack, publication/calendar visibility and a derived Needs Attention surface.

Internal queue/job complexity remains available only as diagnostics.

Script and CreativePlan remain editable but are not extra mandatory approval gates.

## Consequences
- lower operational burden;
- review throughput remains high;
- exceptions stay visible;
- UI remains aligned with user-as-creative-director rather than user-as-workflow-orchestrator.
